Privacy Policy
Last updated: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how iLedgy ("iLedgy", "we", "us", or "our"), operating the iLedgy application, a personal productivity software application designed for record-keeping ("Platform" or "App"), collects, uses, discloses, stores, and protects your personal data when you access or use our services. iLedgy is a record-keeping and organisational productivity tool; it does not provide certified investment advisory or fiduciary financial planning services, and no advisory or fiduciary relationship arises from your use of the Platform. This Policy is published in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules"), and, where applicable, the guidelines issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India ("SEBI") and the Reserve Bank of India ("RBI") relating to the handling of financial and securities-related data.

Category Classification: iLedgy is a personal productivity software application (Primary Category: Productivity; Secondary Category: Finance).

By registering an account, accessing, or using the Platform, you expressly consent to the collection, processing, storage, transfer, and disclosure of your personal data as described in this Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Policy, you should not use the Platform.

1. Data Fiduciary
For the purposes of the DPDP Act, iLedgy acts as the "Data Fiduciary" — the entity that, alone or in conjunction with others, determines the purpose and means of processing your personal data. The Data Fiduciary can be contacted using the details provided in the "Grievance Officer and Contact" section of this Policy.

2. Information We Collect
We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

2.1 Identity and Contact Data: Full name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, and country of residence, which are collected at the time of registration and used for account creation, authentication, and communication.

2.2 Account and Preference Data: User-selected display name, preferred currency, theme preference, and application configuration settings.

2.3 Financial and Portfolio Data: Information you voluntarily input relating to your assets, liabilities, investments, holdings, transactions, budget entries, and associated metadata including, where provided, stock tickers, quantities, average prices, current values, and due dates. This data is provided by you for the purpose of portfolio tracking and analytics.

2.4 Technical and Usage Data: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, device identifiers, time zone settings, operating system, and interaction data such as pages visited and features used, collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies.

2.5 Authentication Data: Login credentials and, where third-party authentication (such as Google) is used, the limited profile information shared by the identity provider.

2.6 Quiz Competition and Prize Verification Data: Where you participate in and emerge as a winner of a live personal finance quiz competition conducted by iLedgy, we may collect such additional verification details as are required to disburse prize money and comply with applicable tax obligations, including your Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Know-Your-Customer (KYC) information. This data is collected solely for prize disbursement, identity verification, and statutory tax deduction at source (TDS) compliance, and is processed in accordance with the SPDI Rules and the Income-tax Act, 1961.

3. How We Use Your Data
We process your personal data for the following lawful purposes:

3.1 Service Provision: To create and manage your account, display your consolidated portfolio, compute net worth, perform currency conversion, and deliver the budgeting and calculator features of the Platform.

3.2 Performance of a Contract: To fulfil our obligations arising from your use of the Platform, including the storage and retrieval of your portfolio and budget entries.

3.3 Market Data Retrieval: To retrieve indicative market prices for securities and other instruments you track, using automated lookups, solely to display estimated values within your portfolio.

3.4 Communication: To respond to your enquiries, send service-related notifications, security alerts, and administrative messages.

3.5 Security and Fraud Prevention: To monitor for unauthorised access, detect and prevent fraud, and protect the integrity of the Platform.

3.6 Compliance: To comply with applicable Indian laws, regulatory requirements, and lawful requests from authorities.

3.7 Quiz Competitions and Prize Administration: To administer live personal finance quiz competitions conducted by iLedgy, verify the identity and eligibility of winners, disburse prize money, and comply with applicable tax-deduction-at-source (TDS) obligations under the Income-tax Act, 1961 in respect of prize payouts.

3.8 Market News Tracking: To retrieve and display market updates and movement insights curated from publicly available sources based on the categories of holdings you track on the Platform, for general informational purposes only and not as investment advice.

3.9 AI Assistant: To process the questions you submit, and any files you voluntarily attach, to the Platform's optional AI Assistant feature, and to generate descriptive summaries and educational commentary based on your records. AI Assistant responses are generated content that may contain errors and do not constitute financial or investment advice. Attachments are stored in your private, access-controlled storage and are handled in accordance with this Policy.

4. Lawful Basis and Consent
Under the DPDP Act, we process your personal data on the basis of (a) your explicit consent, as obtained at the time of registration and through your continued use of the Platform; (b) the necessity of processing for the performance of a contract to which you are a party; and (c) compliance with a legal obligation under Indian law. Where we rely on consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to such withdrawal.

5. Sensitive Personal and Financial Data
Certain data processed by the Platform, including financial portfolio information and investment details, may constitute "Sensitive Personal Data or Information" under the SPDI Rules. We do not collect, store, or process your bank account numbers, card details, net-banking credentials, or trading account passwords. You are not required to, and should not, input such information into the Platform. All portfolio data entered is self-reported by you and remains under your control.

The Platform is a productivity software application designed for personal record-keeping and does not, by itself, constitute investment advice, financial planning, a recommendation to buy or sell securities, or a solicitation to transact on any exchange. Certified investment advisory, fiduciary financial planning, and SEBI-regulated investment advisory services are not offered through this Platform, and iLedgy does not act as a fiduciary or investment adviser with respect to any data you enter. Any market data or market news displayed is indicative and obtained from third-party sources (such as public financial news aggregators); we do not warrant its accuracy, completeness, or timeliness, and such information must not be relied upon as the basis for any investment decision.

Where a Permanent Account Number (PAN) or KYC details are collected from a quiz competition winner for prize disbursement and statutory TDS compliance, such information constitutes Sensitive Personal Data or Information under the SPDI Rules and is handled in accordance with the safeguards set out in this Policy and applicable law.

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements.
